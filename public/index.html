THE AFTERHOURS FOUNDATION

The Afterhours Foundation, Inc. (EIN: 42-5215723) is an Indiana nonprofit founded in 2026 to back hacker activity and help curious people make cool things with software, hardware, and art.

We want to make Purdue one of the best places in the world to be a young, ambitious builder. A place where you can follow an idea just because it's interesting, find people who get excited about it, and make something you're proud of.

Purdue Hackers shows what happens when those people find one another: late nights building with friends, students teaching each other, and ambitious projects made for the joy of it. Afterhours exists to help more people experience that, with the tools, resources, and encouragement to turn an idea into something they can share.

If you'd like to support our work or partner with us, get in touch.
