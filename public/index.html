THE AFTERHOURS FOUNDATION

The Afterhours Foundation, Inc. (EIN: 42-5215723) is an Indiana nonprofit founded in 2026 to support hacker activity: learning, experimenting, and making things with software, hardware, and art.

We want to make West Lafayette one of the best places in the world to be a young, ambitious builder. That starts with giving curious people the tools, resources, and community to work on things they care about, share what they make, and learn from one another.

Our charitable and educational purpose is to help students and other community members pursue projects of their own. That can mean learning to solder, teaching a workshop, experimenting with a new medium, or sharing a project with other people. We aim to make that kind of learning more accessible through mentorship, project resources, and opportunities to build together.

If you'd like to support our work or partner with us, get in touch.
