THE AFTERHOURS FOUNDATION

The Afterhours Foundation, Inc. (EIN: 42-5215723) is an Indiana nonprofit founded in 2026 to back Purdue Hackers, a community of students making cool things with software, hardware, and art.

We want to make Purdue one of the best places in the world to be a young, ambitious builder. That starts with giving curious people the tools, resources, and community to work on things they care about, share what they make, and learn from one another.

Purdue Hackers is built around making things together: Friday nights at Hack Night, workshops run by students, microgrants for ambitious projects, and the end-of-semester Show. Afterhours exists to help fund and organize these programs, so students can spend more time building, learning from each other, and sharing their work.

If you'd like to support our work or partner with us, get in touch.
